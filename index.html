What We Do

Explore the Whole Design Space

Performance · Efficiency · Cost · Manufacturability

A motor design balances performance, efficiency, cost and manufacturability. Even with modern tools, exploring that trade-off is slow work: set up a sweep, wait for the solver, sift the results, adjust, go again. The deadline decides how much of the design space you cover.

GoSim covers more of it. The method is patent-pending.
