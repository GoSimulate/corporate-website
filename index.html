What We Do

Beyond Parametric Design

Simulation · Optimisation · Geometry

Most engineering design optimisation relies on parameterised geometry and predefined design templates. These approaches can identify strong solutions, but they also constrain the design space to concepts that have already been anticipated.

GoSim is developing a new patent-pending approach to computational design that allows geometry to evolve more freely, while maintaining the constraints required for engineering feasibility and manufacture.

By combining advanced simulation, optimisation and a fundamentally different way of representing geometry, we aim to help engineers explore a broader design space and discover better solutions.
